# Online authorization for support account observations

Audit/Operations brokers support account status observations because it owns incidents, expiring grants and the retained disclosure record. ADMIN issues a versioned, temporary ACCOUNT_STATUS_READ grant to the immutable incident creator and resource. Admission and final disclosure are separate short database transactions around the bounded Account call: expiry, revocation or resolution during that call withholds its result. Reopening requires a new grant. All decisions use the database clock; no account contents enter audit facts.

This extends ADR 0011's consumer-only role with a dedicated `fincore-audit` machine identity. Account independently requires that signed client and `account.support.read` for a redacted internal status route. Dynamic per-incident authorization is centralized at the broker rather than copied into Account or encoded in a long-lived browser token. Consequently a compromised broker identity could inspect redacted account status outside a grant; it cannot search accounts, read balances or journals, mutate accounts, or move funds. Audit holds no Account database or Keycloak administration credential.

The final committed check is the disclosure decision, not a distributed financial snapshot or a revocation of already delivered bytes. Failure to retain that decision withholds the response. Denied attempts on an existing incident are retained without account payloads. HTTP authentication/role failures occur before this domain check. Grants share the actor-global command receipt namespace with incident mutations; replay returns the original receipt, not current permission.
