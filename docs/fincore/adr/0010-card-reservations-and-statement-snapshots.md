# ADR 0010: Ledger reservations and immutable statement snapshots

Status: accepted and implemented; [Phase 7 acceptance evidence](../../verification/phase-7.md).

Card is an independent workflow and projection service. Its synthetic `SIM` label cannot function as a payment credential. Ledger owns the card spending guards, immutable canonical authorization receipts, live reservations, action audit and capture/refund journals. No Card database balance authorizes spending.

Every existing debit and every reservation takes the same account locks. Available funds equal booked funds minus unexpired HELD reservations. Reserving and releasing do not invent journals. Capture removes its own reservation and posts atomically; the journal deadline trigger rechecks expiry at the posting point. Account restrictions still apply. Freezing a card blocks new holds, while an already admitted unexpired merchant authorization remains capturable. Termination requires release or expiry of all live holds.

The transaction limit and UTC daily limit are checked at admission. Daily usage counts captures/refunds and live holds admitted that day. A refund does not replenish the daily limit. Full capture, release and full exact reversal are supported; partial amounts and real merchant networks are excluded. A definitive failed refund remains visible and cannot be retried under a fresh financial identity.

Only the signed CUSTOMER delegated through `fincore-card` may create/configure cards, prepare merchant instructions or settle them. The Card worker has inspection/reservation scopes and no customer management role. Fraud reads Card's canonical context through `card.risk.read`; merchant spending receives financial rules through a separate `fraud.card.assess` path. Ledger independently validates the bound short-lived ALLOW before holding funds. There is no permissive outage fallback.

Before an admission request, Card durably records the last grant deadline under a fenced lease. After a lost response, it first reads the Ledger receipt. If no hold is visible, it waits for that grant to expire and calls a Ledger recovery barrier under the admission account lock. A previous uncommitted admission must finish before that barrier returns. Only then can a later BLOCK or REVIEW resolve a genuinely absent hold. No browser JWT is retained in the workflow database. Recovery scans immutable Ledger control/action receipts, so intermediate freezes survive a response gap; Card publishes idempotent outbox events for every discovered version. A recovered historical freeze may notify once without regressing Notification's current version watermark.

Reporting exchanges the signed CUSTOMER token for `ledger.statement.read`; its machine identity only has account lookup and reconciliation. Ledger independently checks the actual account owner and produces one REPEATABLE READ snapshot. Opening plus signed period postings must equal closing. Current booked/reserved/available funds and the observation time are separate from historical month-end booked amounts. A month over 1,000 entries is explicitly refused with `REPORT_TOO_LARGE`; it is never silently truncated.

Reporting persists immutable snapshots and a SHA-256 of canonical JSON content, excluding only the observation timestamp for replay. Changes in balances, live reservations, ledger version or postings produce distinct snapshots. JSON, CSV and PDF read the same owner-scoped snapshot. CSV neutralizes formula-like text while preserving exact signed monetary strings. PDF includes full journal references, amounts, running balances and source hash with explicit pagination. The implementation pins [Apache PDFBox 3.0.8](https://pdfbox.apache.org/3.0/getting-started.html). The generated multi-page export is rendered and visually inspected as part of local validation.

Finance reconciliation requires an explicit FINANCE_OPERATOR at Gateway and Reporting. ADMIN alone does not inherit it. Card and Reporting each have their own database, migrator and bounded runtime permissions. The milestone does not introduce Phase 8 operational administration, a dedicated Audit service or telemetry dashboards.
